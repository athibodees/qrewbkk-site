Join QREW Zero — the founding beta

FOR CLIENTS

Browse verified crew with real portfolios and published day rates. Send a booking request with your brief; get a quotation, confirm, and receive proper documents — quotation, booking confirmation, tax invoice — issued for every job.

One person can be booked in several roles on the same job — a photographer who retouches is one booking, not two.

FOR CREW

Your rate is your rate — QREW adds its service fee on top for the client, never out of your fee. Withholding tax is handled with a ทวิ 50 certificate on every payout, and your calendar, rates and portfolio live in one place.

Gear owners can list cameras, lenses and lighting for rental with deposits and delivery handled inside the booking.

HOW IT WORKS

Request → quote → confirm → shoot → wrap. Every step is recorded: the brief, the agreed price, usage rights, call time and the paper trail — so both sides always know what was agreed.

Currently in closed beta with a founding group of Bangkok crew (QREW Zero). Public launch to follow.
