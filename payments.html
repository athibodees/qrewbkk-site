Payments, cancellation & refunds

Last updated 12 September 2026 · applies to bookings made in the QREW bkk app

What you are paying for

QREW bkk is a booking platform for creative crew in Bangkok — photographers, videographers, stylists, makeup artists, digitechs, assistants and the other roles listed on the home page. A client books a crew member for a job with agreed dates, times and a brief. The service is delivered in person on the shoot day (or as post-production work such as retouching and editing).

Price

  • Crew set their own rate. QREW adds a service fee of 20% of that rate on top. The total shown before you send a request is the full amount; nothing is added later.
  • Prices shown to clients include VAT. A quotation and, once paid, a receipt / tax invoice are issued in the app for every payment.
  • Company clients deduct 3% withholding tax against White Glove Company Limited and receive the certificate; individuals pay the total as shown.
  • Overtime and extras agreed during the job are proposed in the app and become payable only after the other side approves them.

How and when you pay

  • Payment is made inside the QREW bkk app at the "Pay to confirm" step, after the crew member has accepted your request and you have approved the price on the booking confirmation.
  • Payment methods: Thai QR PromptPay, credit and debit cards, and mobile banking, processed by Omise (Opn Payments), a licensed payment service provider in Thailand. QREW does not store card numbers.
  • The booking becomes confirmed the moment the payment is received. Funds are held by White Glove Company Limited and released to the crew member after the job is confirmed complete.
  • Any approved overtime or extra is paid the same way, as a separate balance payment, before the job is closed.

Cancellation by the client

A confirmed (paid) booking can be cancelled from the app. The refund depends on how much notice is given before the job's start time:

Notice before the startRefund of the amount paid
More than 72 hours100%
Between 24 and 72 hours50%
Less than 24 hoursNo refund

Before payment, a request can be withdrawn at any time at no cost. Requests that receive no reply expire after 72 hours.

Cancellation by the crew member

If the crew member cancels after accepting, or does not turn up, the client is refunded in full, including the service fee, and the cancellation counts against the crew member's profile.

Changes to dates

Either side can propose new dates for a confirmed job in the app. Nothing changes until the other side accepts; the booking confirmation is then reissued. Moving a job is not a cancellation and carries no charge.

How refunds are made

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method (the PromptPay account, card or bank account used to pay).
  • We initiate the refund within 3 business days of the cancellation being accepted in the app. Banks and card issuers typically take a further 5–10 business days to show it.
  • You receive an in-app notification and an email when the refund is initiated. Tax documents are reissued to match.

Problems with a job

If something goes wrong on the day, report it from the booking in the app (Help & support) and you receive a case number. We review the job record, messages and documents and answer by email, normally within one working day. Disputes are resolved by White Glove Company Limited under the Terms of service.
